## Implementation Recommendations for Dealenz

### Must-Have (Immediate)
1. **Add "Resources" to main navigation** (Platform, Solutions, Integrations, **Resources**, Pricing, Security, Company)
2. **Restructure Resources hub** with: Blog, Customer Stories, Guides, Events, Help Center, API Docs, Templates
3. **Move Blog/Insights to Resources** — currently at `/insights/[slug]` but not discoverable
4. **Create 3 lead magnet guides** for mid-funnel capture:
   - **"Legal AI Maturity Assessment"** (like LinkSquares) — 4-pillar self-assessment
   - **"Contract Risk Playbook: 50 Clauses Every GC Must Review"** — practical reference
   - **"Build vs. Buy: Contract Intelligence Platform Evaluation Guide"** — buyer's guide

### Should-Have (Q1)
5. **Launch Templates Library** — 10-20 contract templates (MSA, NDA, Lease, SOW, etc.) with Dealenz rulepack annotations
6. **Publish 3 Customer Case Studies** with quantified outcomes (anonymized if needed)
7. **Add Events/Webinars section** — monthly "Contract Intelligence Office Hours", quarterly "Legal AI Trends"
8. **Create "By Use Case" solution pages** (MSA Review, Vendor Onboarding, Lease Audit, Renewal Management, Audit Prep) — 5-10 pages
9. **Technical deep-dive page** — "How Dealenz's Rulepack Engine Works" (like Lexion's AI page)

### Nice-to-Have (Q2)
10. **Sirion University-style certification** — "Dealenz Certified Contract Analyst" course
11. **Interactive ROI Calculator** — "Calculate time savings for your team"
12. **Legal AI Maturity Assessment Tool** — interactive version of guide
13. **Regulatory Compliance Guides** — GDPR, HIPAA, SOX contract implications
14. **Partner/Integration Guides** — "Connecting Dealenz to Salesforce", "Word Add-in Setup"
15. **Changelog** — product velocity signal (like Linear, Vercel)
