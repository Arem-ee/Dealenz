1. **Add scale metrics to Hero/Trust Strip** — even if modest: "X contracts analyzed", "Y risks identified", "Z companies onboarded"

3. **Add customer logo strip** to Hero (below fold) and Footer — even 3-5 logos with permission
4. **Quantify 2-3 key outcomes** from beta/early usage: "Average 45 min saved per contract review", "92% risk detection accuracy", "3x faster turnaround"
