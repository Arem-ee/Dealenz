## Key Patterns Observed (General SaaS Best Practices)

### Trust & Security
- **Quantified social proof at scale:** 100M users, 50 countries, 50% YC companies, 1.4M community, 62% Fortune 100
- **G2 #1 ranking** (3 years) — third-party validation
- **Trust Center** in nav
- **Security page** in Company nav
- **Explicit compliance details:** AES-256, TLS 1.2+, zero data retention, no training on customer data
- **Enterprise features listed explicitly:** SCIM, audit log, DLP/SIEM, SAML SSO, domain management, private teamspaces

### Interactive Demo
- **"Take a self-guided demo of a Notion workspace"** — interactive product experience
- **"See what Notion can do"** with clickable use case demos (Slack tickets, security alerts, weekly reporting)
- **"Start with the Free Plan"** — freemium self-serve
- **Templates** as evaluation accelerator

### Buyer Journey / Persona-Based
- **Solutions by Team:** Engineering, Design, Product, Marketing, Sales, HR, Operations — 7 roles
- **By Use Case** — additional segmentation
- **Enterprise page** separate from main product page
- **Pricing page** with clear tier comparison including Enterprise

### Social Proof
- **Massive quantified metrics:** 100M users, 50 countries, 50% YC, 1.4M community, 62% Fortune 100
- **G2 #1 for 3 years** — sustained third-party validation
- **Community size** (1.4M) as ecosystem proof
- **Customer Stories** in Resources (implied)

### Technical Credibility
- **AI governance details:** Zero data retention, no training on customer data
- **Security specifics:** AES-256, TLS 1.2+, SCIM, audit log, DLP/SIEM, SAML SSO
- **API Docs** in Resources
- **Developer/technical content** in Guides/Community
- **Integrations page** as first-class product feature

### SEO/Content Strategy
- **Templates** as major SEO asset (implied by Templates in Product nav)
- **Guides, Community, Blog, Events, Help Center, API Docs**
- **Team/Use Case solutions** as SEO landing pages
- **Enterprise guide** ("The ultimate quickstart guide to Notion for Enterprise") — long-form content

### Pricing
- **Transparent pricing page** with 4 tiers
- **Enterprise features listed explicitly** (not "contact us for details")
- **Free tier** as entry point
- **Clear upgrade path** from Free → Plus → Business → Enterprise
