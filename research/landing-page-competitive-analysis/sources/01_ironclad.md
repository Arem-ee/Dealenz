## Key Patterns Observed

### Trust & Security (CONFIRMED - H1)
- **Explicit compliance badges:** SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA listed on enterprise page
- **Gartner/Forrester analyst recognition** prominently displayed in hero AND enterprise page
- **Security section** with specific infrastructure details (Google Cloud, multi-region)
- **Certifications** called out as bullet points, not just mentioned in passing

### Interactive Demo/Sandbox (CONFIRMED - H2)
- **"Watch demo video"** CTA in hero
- **"Take a self-guided demo"** mentioned on Notion (but Ironclad has "Book a demo" primary)
- **Product screenshots** with annotations showing actual UI
- **Workflow Designer** described as "no-code UI to build and launch contract processes in minutes" — implies interactive capability

### Buyer Journey / Role-Based Entry (CONFIRMED - H3)
- **Solutions by Team:** Legal Ops, Procurement, Sales — each with dedicated pages
- **By Use Case** and **By Industry** navigation
- **Role-specific value props** on each solution page (e.g., Legal Ops gets "self-serve contract management", Sales gets "accelerate sales cycles")

### Social Proof (CONFIRMED - H4)
- **Named analyst recognition:** Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader 2025, Forrester Wave Leader Q1 2025
- **Customer logo strip:** "The world's leading companies trust Ironclad"
- **Customer stories section** in Resources nav
- **Quantified claims:** "Over 2 billion contracts managed", "5,000+ customers" (on Gong but similar pattern)

### Technical Credibility / Integrations (CONFIRMED - H5)
- **Dedicated Integrations page** in main nav
- **30+ integrations** called out with specific names: Salesforce, Coupa, Ramp, Slack
- **"Deepest integrations in the market"** claim with "number one Salesforce integration"
- **Public APIs** mentioned for custom integrations
- **Integration logos** in hero area and dedicated section

### SEO/Content Strategy (CONFIRMED - H6)
- **Resources hub** with: Blog, Events, Guides, Customer Stories, Help Center, API Docs
- **FAQ section** on homepage ("What Is Contract Management?")
- **Educational content** integrated into product pages
- **Guides and whitepapers** as lead magnets

### Pricing Transparency (CONFIRMED - H7)
- **Pricing in main nav** (not hidden)
- **Enterprise focus:** "Contact Sales" pattern for enterprise, but pricing page exists
- **Credit-based/usage model** not visible — appears to be traditional enterprise subscription
