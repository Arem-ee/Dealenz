## Hypotheses to Re-Test on Refresh

### H1: Trust/Security Compliance
- **Re-test:** Have any competitors added/removed compliance badges? (e.g., ISO 27001, FedRAMP)
- **Trigger:** New certification announcement, Trust Center update

### H2: Interactive Demos
- **Re-test:** Has any pure-enterprise competitor (Gong, Sirion, LinkSquares) added self-serve trial?
- **Trigger:** "Start Free" or "Try Now" CTA appearance

### H3: Persona-Based Entry
- **Re-test:** Have Evisort or Sirion added explicit Solutions by Team nav?
- **Trigger:** Navigation restructuring

### H4: Social Proof
- **Re-test:** New named case studies with quantified outcomes? Video testimonials?
- **Trigger:** Customer Stories section update, new case study publication

### H5: Technical Credibility
- **Re-test:** New named integrations (especially CRM, Word, E-sign)? MCP Server / AI Agent Skills?
- **Trigger:** Integrations page update, Developer blog post

### H6: SEO/Content
- **Re-test:** New lead magnets (maturity assessments, guides, templates)? Academy/University?
- **Trigger:** Resources section update, new guide publication

### H7: Pricing Transparency
- **Re-test:** Has any pure-enterprise competitor added pricing page or Enterprise tier details?
- **Trigger:** Pricing page addition, Enterprise feature list publication
