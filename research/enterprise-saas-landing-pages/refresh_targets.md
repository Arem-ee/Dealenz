## Adversarial Triggers (Re-run Research If)

1. **Major Competitor Redesign** — Any competitor launches v2 homepage with new structure
2. **New Market Entrant** — Well-funded legal tech startup launches with novel landing pattern
3. **Regulatory Change** — New AI regulation (EU AI Act enforcement, US state laws) changes compliance messaging requirements
4. **Analyst Report Shift** — Gartner MQ / Forrester Wave repositioning changes leader landscape
5. **Dealenz Funding/Stage Change** — Series A/B/C changes buyer expectations (startup → scale-up → enterprise)
6. **Product Pivot** — Dealenz adds/removes major capabilities (e.g., adds CLM workflow, removes signing)
