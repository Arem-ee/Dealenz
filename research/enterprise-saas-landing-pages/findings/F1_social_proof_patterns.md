## Implementation Recommendations for Dealenz

### Must-Have (Priority 1)
1. **Customer Logo Row** — Add "Trusted by [X] companies" with 6-8 recognizable logos above the fold or in TrustStrip replacement
2. **Named Testimonials Section** — 3-4 testimonials with photo, name, title, company, specific outcome quote
3. **Quantified Impact Metrics** — Even if early: "X contracts analyzed", "Y% risk findings accuracy", "Z hours saved per review"
4. **G2/Capterra Badges** — If listed, embed badges; if not, prioritize getting listed

### Should-Have (Priority 2)
5. **Case Study Section** — "See how [Customer] achieved [Result]" with download links
6. **Analyst/Expert Validation** — Advisory board members, legal tech analyst quotes, law firm partner quotes
7. **Review Platform Links** — "Read our reviews on G2" with direct link

### Nice-to-Have (Priority 3)
8. **Customer Count/Scale** — "Used by legal teams at [X] companies" when meaningful
9. **Video Testimonials** — Short clips (30-60s) for higher engagement
