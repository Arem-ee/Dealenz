## Insaim Framework Validation

Per Insaim (Source 30), enterprise/compliance products require **Awareness Ladder** or **Problem-First** pattern where "every screen answers exactly one question" and "the visitor is never asked to hold multiple ideas simultaneously."

Content hub serves as **credibility infrastructure** — buyers in regulated industries (legal tech) verify vendor maturity through content depth before engaging sales.

## Implementation Recommendations for Dealenz

### Must-Have (Priority 1)
1. **Interactive Assessment Tool** — "Legal AI Maturity Assessment" (direct response to LinkSquares) — 10-question diagnostic → personalized report → lead capture
2. **Resource Library Hub** — `/resources` with categories: Guides, Webinars, Templates, Case Studies, Reports — filterable by role/industry
3. **Playbook/Template Gallery** — "Dealenz Rulepack Library" — downloadable playbooks for: MSA Review, Lease Audit, Vendor Agreement, NDA, SOW, Employment Agreement — gated or ungated

### Should-Have (Priority 2)
4. **Webinar Program** — Monthly "Contract Intelligence Live" — topics: "Deterministic vs Generative AI", "Building Legal Playbooks", "Obligation Tracking Best Practices" — recorded → on-demand library
5. **Buyer's Guide** — "How to Evaluate Contract Intelligence Platforms" — vendor-neutral framework positioning Dealenz advantages
6. **Changelog / Release Notes** — Monthly "What's New in Dealenz" — signals velocity, builds trust (Moda finding: "changelog-style section signaling product velocity")

### Nice-to-Have (Priority 3)
7. **Podcast/Video Series** — "Contract Intelligence Conversations" — interview GCs, Legal Ops leaders, Procurement heads
8. **Community/Forum** — Slack community or forum for legal ops professionals
9. **Certification Program** — "Dealenz Certified Legal Operations" — builds ecosystem
