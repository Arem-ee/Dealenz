> **Section 1: Introduce novelty, then restore comprehension**

> "Attio customer section claiming support from first agent through enterprise scale. Attio uses customer count and logos to argue that the product can span company stages."
> "The customer section says 'Trusted by 30,000+ customers' and 'From first agent to enterprise scale,' then shows customer logos."

### Linear: Let One Product Story Carry the Whole Page
> "Linear states three evaluation principles before presenting deeper product workflows."
> "Linear structures its middle as deep product-development chapters rather than many shallow feature cards."
> "The page moves through intake and integrations, planning and monitoring, AI and automations, and build, review, and ship."
> "Subsequent sections introduce Stack, accounting workflows, automated access, global spend, customer receipts, and a final CTA."

### Beehiiv: Pricing Architecture as Maturity Ladder
> "Beehiiv uses pricing architecture to make its customer maturity ladder explicit."
> "The 'Build your way' section exposes a subscriber slider, monthly or yearly choice, and four plan levels: Launch, Scale, Max, and Enterprise. This is not merely pricing."

### Enterprise Move: Customer Scale as Product Mechanism
> "This is a distinctive enterprise move: customer scale is presented not only as proof of adoption, but as part of the product mechanism. The claim is powerful and therefore deserves careful support."

## Analysis Notes

**Validates H1:** Customer logos + count + "From first agent to enterprise scale" narrative = scale argument, not just social proof

**Validates H3:** Linear's "deep product-development chapters rather than many shallow feature cards" — Dealenz's Advantage tabs are shallow feature cards; needs deeper workflow chapters

**Validates H4:** Attio "addresses the setup objection created by its ambitious promise" — proactive objection handling; Dealenz doesn't address implementation/setup objections

**Validates H5:** Changelog-style section "signaling product velocity" — Dealenz has no changelog/velocity signal

**Validates H6:** Pricing as "customer maturity ladder" (Launch/Scale/Max/Enterprise) — Dealenz has simple pay-per-deal; enterprise buyers expect tiered maturity progression

**Key Insight:** Enterprise pages build arguments in layers: Novelty → Objection Handling → Scale Proof → Velocity Signal → Pricing as Maturity Ladder → Final CTA
